# Our original buddy sprites (v1, hand-drawn 2026-06-12)

Archive of the first hand-drawn sprite set. buddy.js SPECIES currently uses
these frames for most species; dragon and capybara were later redrawn (v2).
Restore any species by copying its poses back into buddy.js SPECIES.
