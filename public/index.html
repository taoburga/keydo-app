            quick-add syntax

            • !high · !mid · !low · !!! = high · !! = med · ! = low — priority (a spaced-out ! only; done! stays punctuation)
            • tomorrow · friday · this weekend · next week · may 3 · in a week · end of month · 2026-05-15 — due date, typed anywhere. Add a time: friday 1pm · tomorrow morning · tonight
            • prefix with ? to force a date the bare words skip: ?sat (short day names) · ?27th · ?3 may · ?mid may · ?5/15 · ?eod · ?eow
            • ls:<name> — send to a different list (e.g. ls:todos, ls:onhold); fuzzy-matched, falls back to current list if no match
            • #tag — auto-aggregates in sidebar; #flag reserved for ★ flag

                data & privacy
                
      
                
        
                tasks live in Apple Reminders (synced by iCloud) — this app is a front-end. it has no servers, no accounts and no telemetry: nothing is sent anywhere on its behalf, except fetching the page title of a link you save to your reading list.
                
        
                app data (trash, history, briefings) sits in ~/Library/Application Support/todo-app. deleted tasks are recoverable for 7 days.
                
        
                Claude connects through a local MCP server: every change it makes is written to an audit log, and its deletes land in the same trash.
